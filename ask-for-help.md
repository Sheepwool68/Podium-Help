# Asking RFID Timing for help, or for something to be built

On the website: the `Support` tab.

It is raised from the account the club is signed in to, so the club, its boxes and what it has timed
are already known. There is nothing to describe about the setup and no reference number to quote.

## The two kinds

Two buttons, and they go to the same place — the difference is what is wanted:

- **`Report a problem`** — something is not working, or is not doing what was expected. Say what you
  were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. There is no need to describe the boxes
  or the events; those are already visible from the account.
- **`Can you build me that?`** — Podium does not do something, and you would like it to. Say what
  you would like it to do and when you would use it. The answer says what can be done and roughly
  when, and it is a real answer rather than a queue number.

Give it a one-line subject and write the request, then `Send`.

## What happens next

An acknowledgement appears straight away, in the thread, saying what the account shows — the boxes,
the events, the entrants and the reads on record. It is there so you can see the request arrived
against the right club.

**Replies appear in the same thread, and are emailed to the club's contact email**, so there is no
need to sit on the page. Set that address under `Club` if it is not right.

You can add to a thread at any time — the reply box is at the bottom of it. Anything added lands on
the same conversation rather than starting a new one.

## During a race

**Do not wait on this if a race is running.** A request is read by a person, not answered by a
machine, so it is the right route for "this is wrong and we need it fixed" and the wrong route for
"the finish mat has stopped and the field is coming". For that, work the fault: the pages here on a
box that will not connect, a box connected with nothing coming through, or reads that stopped part
way through are the fastest way back to timing the race.

Whatever happens, **the reads are safe** — they are kept on the box and can be fetched again
afterwards. See [recover-missed-reads.md](recover-missed-reads.md) and
[back-everything-up.md](back-everything-up.md).

## From Podium PC and Podium Mobile

Those two have a `Help` button of their own, which answers from this same help and can copy the
whole of it out for an assistant to read. A request raised to RFID Timing is raised on the website.
